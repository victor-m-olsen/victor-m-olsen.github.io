Border Requirements

  • Driver must complete TEP (Temporary Export Permit) – MK10,000.
  • Present blue book (vehicle registration).
  • If not owner: provide letter of consent (driver's name, passport no., car reg.).
  • Visa-exempt countries: UK, USA, EU, Canada, Norway, Australia, China, Japan, South Korea, Gulf States.
  • Visas available at border but recommended in advance.
  • Driver must complete TIP (Temporary Import Permit).
  • Third-party insurance (~$40) required.
  • Carbon emission tax: 150 Kwacha (~$25).
  • Chipata Municipal Council fee: 40 Kwacha (~$5).
  • Interpol Clearance from police required for vehicle.
